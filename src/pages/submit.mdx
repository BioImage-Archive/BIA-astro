## Submitting data to the BioImage Archive and EMPIAR

Currently, submission cannot be initiated directly from this website. 

* To deposit data to the BioImage Archive, please visit the BIA [submission page](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/bioimage-archive/submit/).

* To submit to EMPIAR, please use EMPIAR's [deposition system](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/empiar/deposition/choose-action/).

Unsure which repository is best for your data? Find more information [here](https://beta.bioimagearchive.org/bioimage-archive/policies/imagesatebi).
