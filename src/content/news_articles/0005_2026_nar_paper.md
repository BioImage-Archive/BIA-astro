In this new paper, we present the latest developments in the BioImage Archive, including infrastructure that brings together imaging data across modalities and scales, improved tools for searching, exploring and visualising datasets, interactive viewing without downloading entire datasets, and more than 11 petabytes of imaging data across the BioImage Archive and EMPIAR ecosystem.
